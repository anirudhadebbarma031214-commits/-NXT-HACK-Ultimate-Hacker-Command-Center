LEARN BY DOING

Beginner-friendly challenges that require no real targets.

LEVEL 01

Spot the weak password

Why is password123 weak?

Reveal explanation

It is common and predictable. Use a unique long passphrase or password-manager-generated password plus MFA.

LEVEL 02

Read a URL

In https://login.example.com.attacker.test, what is the host?

Reveal explanation

The host is login.example.com.attacker.test; the final domain is attacker.test. Read hostnames right to left.

LEVEL 03

Hash or encryption?

Can SHA-256 be decrypted like encrypted text?

Reveal explanation

No. Hashes are designed to be one-way. Password storage needs salted, slow password-hashing algorithms.

LEVEL 04

Permission first

A website is public. Does that mean you can scan it?

Reveal explanation

No. Public accessibility is not authorization. Get explicit permission and stay in the agreed scope.
